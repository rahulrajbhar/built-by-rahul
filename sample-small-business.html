01 — About Vanguard

Architectural rigor meets reliable craftsmanship.

Founded in 2012 by principal architects Vikram Sen and Ananya Rao, we eliminated the classic headaches of delayed contractors and surprise cost overruns.

Most construction and renovation projects suffer from a disconnect between the design architect and the execution team. At Vanguard, we manage both under one unified contract.

From initial municipal sanction drawings to structural civil work, electrical engineering, custom cabinetry, and final handover, you deal with one dedicated project manager who reports weekly on progress.
